# Crews, instruments and red teams, by domain

The range lever says to name the roles a strong team would have, including from outside the obvious discipline, and to ask for the work each role does rather than the voice it writes in.

Two rules govern every row. **Assign work, not register:** the column says what the role produces, and that is what to ask for. **The red team's job is to find what is wrong with the finished result,** so it runs last and never grades its own side's work.

---

## Forensic accounting and disputed money

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Forensic accountant | Ties every disclosed figure to a bank line and lists what does not tie |
| Short seller | Argues the entity is worth less than claimed and names the three figures that would prove it |
| Coverage analyst | Reads the policy and bond for who must pay and in what order |
| Records custodian | Establishes what a produced set actually contains, and what a missing folder invalidates |
| Deposition preparer | Turns each conclusion into the question it must survive |

**Instrument:** a queryable dataset, not a workbook. A workbook's internal check still passes after an input row is deleted.
**Red team:** the short seller, plus a second accountant reading only the other side's exhibits.
**Outside judge:** the bank line, the recorded document, the counterparty's own record.

## Bookkeeping and the close

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Controller | Decides which account a transaction belongs in and why |
| Tax preparer | Says what each treatment does to the return |
| Auditor | Asks what document supports the entry, and refuses the entry without it |
| Reviewer | Finds the reclassification nobody authorised |

**Instrument:** the accounting file and the statements as data, not a summary of either.
**Red team:** the auditor, asking for support on every descriptive phrase sitting beside a number.

## Construction and permitting

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Owner's representative | Names which items on the list look closed and are not |
| Plan checker | Reads the submittal as the agency will, and lists what gets returned |
| Scheduler | Puts a date on each dependency and finds the one that moves the opening |
| Cost engineer | Prices the delay rather than describing it |
| Fire and health reviewer | Reads the condition of approval nobody else reads |

**Instrument:** the permit portal record and the inspection history, not the tracker built from them.
**Red team:** the plan checker, plus a lender's fund-control reviewer asking what the draw actually bought.

## Land, entitlement and a joint venture

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Land-use planner | Establishes which authority actually governs, before anything else |
| Utility analyst | Says what the parcel can be served with, and at what cost |
| Deal partner | Writes the case against putting capital in |
| Existing operator | Says what the current use earns while entitlement runs |

**Red team:** the deal partner, holding whatever capital gate the venture set.

## Adjudication

Used carefully, because the crew cannot include an advocate. The roles are analytic only.

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Research attorney | Verifies every citation to a pinpoint page and flags anything unverified |
| Appellate reviewer | Reads the draft for the ground it would be reversed on |
| Unrepresented reader | Says which sentence a party without counsel would not understand |

**Outside judge:** the authority itself, read in the reporter, never a summary. A citator is not optional, and nothing published is confirmed as current law without one.

## Advocacy and filings

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Opposing counsel | Writes the response to the draft |
| The decider's research attorney | Says which argument gets reached and which gets ignored |
| The client | Says whether the document can be followed on one read |

**Red team:** opposing counsel.

## Publishing

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Acquiring editor | Gives the accept or decline, with the reason |
| Line editor | Fixes the sentence rather than describing the problem |
| Continuity reader | Finds what contradicts the established canon |
| Comparable-title analyst | Reads live listings for what the market already rewards |
| Cover designer | Tests whether it reads at thumbnail size |

**Instrument:** the manuscript file itself, word count measured from it with the hash recorded.
**Red team:** the acquiring editor saying no.
**Altitude does not apply to the prose.** Use range and depth here and leave the voice alone.

## Software product and release

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Live-ops lead | Names the metric, the baseline and the window before any change |
| Release engineer | Builds from the branch actually in use and reports the real versions |
| Policy reviewer | Reads the listing as the store's reviewer will |
| Domain expert | Says which claim cannot be made |
| Statistician | Says when the volume cannot support a conclusion |

**Red team:** the policy reviewer and the statistician, the latter empowered to say the data proves nothing.

## Consumer brand

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Regulatory reviewer | Strikes every claim the category cannot make |
| Trademark searcher | Reports each check separately, and never says cleared |
| Category buyer | Says what makes this get picked up over the next item on the shelf |
| Conversion analyst | Traces the live path and names where someone leaves |

**Red team:** the regulatory reviewer.

## Operations and automation

| Role | The work it does |
|---|---|
| Audience researcher | Says who is actually there, from platform data |
| Channel operator | Confirms a thing published, by reading the field that carries the failure |
| Reliability engineer | Asks what breaks silently and leaves the log green |

**Red team:** the reliability engineer, whose standing question is which green log describes nothing. A scheduled job pointed at a frozen input reports success forever.

## Roles worth borrowing into almost anything

The outside-the-discipline picks the range lever is really about.

- **The short seller.** Argues the position is worth less than claimed, and names the figure that settles it. Works on a dispute, a deal, a product and a book.
- **The journalist.** Finds the one fact that changes the story, and asks who benefits.
- **The editor.** Cuts what is not load-bearing, including from an analysis.
- **The reliability engineer.** Asks which success message is reporting nothing.
- **The statistician.** Says when the sample cannot answer the question.
- **The motion and audio designer.** Only when the work will be presented rather than read.
